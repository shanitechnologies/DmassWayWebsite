FAQ

Questions we are often asked

How long does it take?

It depends on the bank, your documents and the facility. We give you a realistic expectation at the start, and we keep you updated.

What documents will I need?

It depends on the facility. After your readiness check we send a clear list. Common ones include your company registration documents, six months of bank statements, financial statements or management accounts from the past year, and proof that your ZIMRA tax affairs are in order.

Do I pay before funding is secured?

Our fee is paid in two parts: 50% to start and 50% when your offer letter is issued. The first payment covers all the preparation work.

Can you guarantee funding?

No one honestly can, because the bank decides. What we guarantee is a thorough, professional application sent to the right lenders.

What if my business isn’t ready?

We tell you straight and show you what to fix.
