Terms of Service

Plain terms for how we work together.

  1. Our serviceWe prepare your financial documents and funding application, identify suitable lenders and guide you through the process.
  2. Funding decisionsApproval and terms are decided by the bank or financial institution. We do not guarantee funding.
  3. FeesFees are paid 50% at the start and 50% when an offer letter is issued. The fee for funding under $100,000 is $3,000. For funding of $100,000 and above, the fee is a percentage of the amount secured, up to 5%, agreed in writing before work begins.
  4. What the deposit coversThe deposit covers the work done to prepare and submit your application, including preparing books and letters and sourcing lenders. It is non-refundable once work has started.
  5. Offer lettersThe balance is due when an offer letter is issued, whether or not the client accepts the offer.
  6. Your responsibilitiesYou agree to give accurate, complete information and to supply documents on time.
  7. Working with other advisersApplications we have prepared and information we have developed are for use with DmassWay only.
  8. TimelinesAny timelines we give are estimates, as they depend on banks and other parties.
  9. Extra workWork outside the agreed scope, such as accounting and ZIMRA services, is quoted separately.
  10. Third-party costsBank charges, interest, legal and valuation fees are paid to those parties.
  11. ConfidentialityWe keep your information and documents confidential.
  12. ComplaintsContact us at admin@dmassway.co.zw and we will respond within 3 working days.
  13. Governing lawThese terms are governed by the laws of Zimbabwe.
